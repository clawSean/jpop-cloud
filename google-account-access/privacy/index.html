JPop Account Access

Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026. JPop Account Access is a private automation operated by Jared Pearson (“JPop”) with his personal AI assistant, Sean. This policy covers its use of Google account data, not other services on jpop.cloud.

Google data accessed and used

With an account owner’s OAuth consent, the automation may access Google Drive files and Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Calendar data. It reads, creates, edits, or deletes content only to carry out requests from an authorized operator. It does not request Gmail access. Google account identity information is used to identify the connected account.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are kept in an encrypted keyring on the operator’s device. Requested content and task results may appear in private assistant conversations and local working records. Those records are retained as needed for the requested work and ongoing operation, and can be deleted on request, subject to any legal obligations.

Sharing and protection

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for their own marketing. To fulfill a requested task, selected data may be processed by the AI or hosting providers used by the assistant. Access to the device and credentials is restricted. Google user data is not used by this app to train generalized AI models.

Control and deletion

An account owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account permissions. To request deletion of locally retained Google account data or ask a privacy question, email pearson.jaredb@gmail.com.

Separate SMS policies

This Google automation does not enroll anyone in SMS. Sean Alerts and other messaging services have separate privacy and terms pages; nothing here changes their terms or consent process.
