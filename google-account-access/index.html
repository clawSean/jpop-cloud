JPop Account Access

This is a private Google account automation for JPop and Sean, his personal AI assistant. It connects only Google accounts that their owners authorize through Google’s consent screen.

What it does

When requested, it can work with Google Drive files, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Calendar events. It does not request Gmail access. The Google permissions granted by each account owner determine what it can see or change.

This page describes the Google account automation only. It is not an SMS sign-up page, and using it does not enroll anyone in Sean Alerts or any text-message program.

Read the Google Account Access privacy policy for details about data use, storage, sharing, and deletion.
