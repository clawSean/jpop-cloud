WHY THIS RULES

“The names aren’t decoration. They tell you who can do what, what happens when they break, and whether anyone should panic.”

The theme makes a complicated system legible. You can explain a trust boundary, a recovery plan, or an ephemeral worker with one character name—and everyone immediately remembers the role.

DOCTOR / BREAK-GLASS RECOVERY

The doctor who stays independent enough to help when Morty’s own system is sick. Beth’s off-host break-glass lane exists to diagnose and recover the primary stack when it cannot rescue itself.

The disposable testing zone. Risky changes can face-plant in Jerry without taking the real operation with them. Nobody wants Jerry hurt; the architecture just plans for it.

Spawned for one job, expected to finish it, and not meant to become permanent infrastructure. A perfect name for short-lived subagents that exist to return a result and disappear.
