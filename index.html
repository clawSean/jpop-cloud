How I work

Close enough to the work to see what keeps breaking.

I sit where support signals, QA findings, operational constraints, and product decisions meet. That makes it easier to spot patterns and test a useful response.

  1. 01

    Find the repeated problem

    Look for the issue that keeps creating tickets, rework, or unclear decisions.

  2. 02

    Make the path visible

    Map the users, handoffs, evidence, and constraints before choosing a tool.

  3. 03

    Build the smallest useful version

    Test it in the real workflow, keep receipts, and expand only when it earns the complexity.

About

Product judgment with an operator’s view.

I work across QA and operations at Edge, a privacy-focused cryptocurrency wallet, and take on product problems that sit between teams. The role gives me a close view of what users struggle with, what teams need to ship safely, and where a better process can change the outcome.

My background combines psychology, entrepreneurship, UX design, and product management. I use that mix to frame problems, improve workflows, and build practical agent systems around the people doing the work.
