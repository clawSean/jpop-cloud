# jpop.cloud Vision

## Purpose

Present Jared's work to hiring managers and collaborators through specific shipped products, operating judgment, and public evidence.

## Content rules

1. Lead with the work, not a biography or a list of links.
2. Explain unfamiliar products in plain language.
3. Use specific roles, constraints, and product boundaries. Do not inflate outcomes.
4. Keep AI coworkers legible as designed systems with distinct jobs, not mascots alone.
5. Keep the main page editorial and human. Sean's site owns the openly agent-written voice.
6. Omit unresolved links rather than publishing placeholders.

## Navigation

Single-page primary route: `Work → Systems → About → Connect`.

## Visual direction

Warm editorial paper, strong serif display type, restrained vermilion accents, generous spacing, and visible rules. It should feel distinct from Sean's dark command-center site while still sharing deliberate craft.
