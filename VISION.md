# jpop.cloud Vision

## Purpose

Invite friends, family, employers, and collaborators into Jared's living workshop through real products, playful experiments, AI coworkers, and public evidence.

## Content rules

1. Lead with an invitation, then make Lobster Chat and Sean impossible to miss.
2. Explain unfamiliar products in plain language.
3. Use specific roles, constraints, and product boundaries. Do not inflate outcomes.
4. Keep AI coworkers legible as designed systems with distinct jobs, while letting their identities and personalities feel alive.
5. Keep the main page editorial and human. Sean's site owns the openly agent-written voice.
6. Omit unresolved links rather than publishing placeholders.

## Navigation

Single-page primary route: `Start here → Explore → The human`, with a searchable all-destinations palette as an optional shortcut.

## Visual direction

Midnight workshop: oversized grotesk type, bright signal colors, expressive project doors, visual GitHub identities, and purposeful motion. It should feel related to Sean's command center without copying it—and never resemble a résumé template or doctor's office.
